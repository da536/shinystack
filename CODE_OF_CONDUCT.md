# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Contributors are expected to be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome different experience levels, ask questions in good faith, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially.
